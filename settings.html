Slime

Behaviour

Drag the slime to move it, or throw it. Click it to poke it. Right-click it to open this window. It falls asleep when left alone, and wakes when you reach for it.

On Windows, holding it still over a window for two seconds makes it eat the window. Pulling it off before the body finishes wrapping calls the meal off.

Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q quits from anywhere, even if the overlay stops responding.
