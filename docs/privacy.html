Privacy Policy

Slime is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, and it sends your data nowhere.

What Slime accesses

With your permission, Slime reads your Google Calendar events through the Google Calendar API, using the single scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly.

From each upcoming event it uses:

  • the event title, to name the reminder;
  • the start time, to decide when to remind you and to count down;
  • the video conferencing link, if the event has one, so that clicking the reminder can open it.

The scope is read-only. Slime cannot create, modify or delete calendar entries, and it requests no access to email, contacts, files or any other Google service.

Where your data goes

  • Nowhere. Calendar data is requested by the application on your computer directly from Google, held in memory to draw the reminder, and discarded. It is not written to disk, not logged, and not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.
  • There is no Slime account, no backend service, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising.
  • The only network destinations the application contacts are Google's own endpoints (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, openidconnect.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com), plus any meeting link you click, which is handed to your default browser.

What is stored, and where

Slime stores your Google sign-in tokens, and the OAuth client credentials it was built with, in the Windows Credential Manager under the name com.slime.desk. They stay on your machine, protected by your Windows user account. No calendar content is stored.

Revoking access

Two independent ways, either of which is sufficient:

  • In Slime, open Settings and press Disconnect. The stored tokens are deleted immediately.
  • At myaccount.google.com/permissions, remove Slime's access. That revokes the tokens at Google's end as well.

Uninstalling the application leaves nothing behind but the credential entry above, which you can delete from Windows Credential Manager.

Children

Slime is a utility for calendar owners and is not directed at children.
