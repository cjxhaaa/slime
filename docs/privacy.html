Privacy Policy

Slime is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, and it sends your data nowhere.

What Slime accesses

Nothing. Slime has no sign-in, reads no accounts, and asks for no permissions beyond running as an ordinary desktop application.

On Windows it can close a window you hold it over. To do that it reads, from windows already open on your own desktop, the title, the position and size, the name of the owning process, and whether Windows reports the window as not responding. That is used to draw and perform the action and is then discarded. It is never written to disk and never leaves your machine.

Where your data goes

  • Nowhere. There is no Slime account, no backend service, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising.
  • The application makes no network requests of its own at all.

What is stored, and where

Nothing is stored. Slime keeps no save file, no configuration file and no credentials, and it forgets everything when it closes.

Children

Slime is a desktop toy and utility, and is not directed at children.
